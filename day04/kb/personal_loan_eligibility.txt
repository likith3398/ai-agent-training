NovaTrust Bank Personal Loan Eligibility
NovaTrust Personal Loans are available to eligible individual applicants.
Applicants must be at least 21 years old.
The maximum applicant age at loan maturity is 60 years.
Applicants must have a minimum monthly income of ₹25,000.
Applicants must have at least 12 months of continuous employment.
Loan approval depends on income, credit history, and internal assessment.
The synthetic maximum personal loan amount is ₹10,00,000.
The minimum personal loan amount is ₹50,000.
Applicants must provide identity, address, and income documents.
Final approval is subject to NovaTrust Bank's internal eligibility assessment.
