NovaTrust Bank Charges Schedule
The NovaTrust savings account minimum balance requirement is ₹1,000.
A physical debit card replacement fee is ₹150.
The annual fee for the Basic Credit Card is ₹500.
The annual fee for the Rewards Credit Card is ₹1,000.
The annual fee for the Travel Credit Card is ₹1,500.
A duplicate account statement request costs ₹50.
A duplicate cheque book request costs ₹100.
The standard NEFT service charge in this synthetic schedule is ₹10.
The standard RTGS service charge is ₹20.
Applicable charges may be reviewed through the NovaTrust service schedule.
