NovaTrust Bank Grievance Escalation Matrix
Customers should first raise complaints with NovaTrust customer support.
Customer support is the first escalation level.
If unresolved after 3 working days, the complaint moves to Level 2.
Level 2 complaints are handled by the Customer Resolution Team.
If unresolved after 7 working days, the complaint moves to Level 3.
Level 3 complaints are handled by the Grievance Officer.
Customers should keep their complaint reference number for follow-up.
The fictional grievance email is grievance@novatrust.example.
Customers should include relevant transaction or complaint details.
Escalation timelines begin from the date the complaint is registered.
