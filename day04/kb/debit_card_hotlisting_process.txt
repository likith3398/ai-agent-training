NovaTrust Bank Debit Card Hotlisting Process
Customers should hotlist a debit card immediately if it is lost or stolen.
The card can be hotlisted through mobile banking or net banking.
Customers can also call the NovaTrust Card Support Desk.
The fictional card support number is 1800-000-2020.
The customer must verify their identity before hotlisting the card.
Hotlisting blocks all further transactions on the affected card.
A hotlisted card cannot be reactivated.
Customers can request a replacement card after hotlisting.
The replacement debit card fee is ₹150.
Replacement cards are normally delivered within 7 working days.
