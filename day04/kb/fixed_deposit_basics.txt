NovaTrust Bank Fixed Deposit Basics
NovaTrust Fixed Deposits can be opened with a minimum deposit of ₹5,000.
The available deposit periods range from 6 months to 60 months.
The synthetic standard interest rate is 6.50% per year.
Interest is calculated according to the selected deposit period.
Customers can choose monthly, quarterly, or maturity interest payout options.
Premature withdrawal may result in reduced interest.
The minimum premature withdrawal period is 3 months.
Fixed Deposits can be opened through net banking or at a NovaTrust branch.
Customers receive a deposit confirmation after the Fixed Deposit is created.
The maturity amount depends on the deposit amount, tenure, and applicable rate.
