NovaTrust Bank Credit Card Types
NovaTrust offers three fictional credit cards: Basic, Rewards, and Travel.
The Basic Card has an annual fee of ₹500.
The Rewards Card has an annual fee of ₹1,000.
The Travel Card has an annual fee of ₹1,500.
The Basic Card provides 1 reward point per ₹100 spent.
The Rewards Card provides 2 reward points per ₹100 spent.
The Travel Card provides 3 reward points per ₹100 spent on travel purchases.
All three cards require successful KYC verification before issuance.
Customers can request a card through NovaTrust net banking or a branch.
