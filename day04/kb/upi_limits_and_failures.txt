NovaTrust Bank UPI Limits and Failures
The maximum UPI transaction amount is ₹50,000 per transaction.
The maximum daily UPI transaction amount is ₹1,00,000.
A customer can make up to 20 successful UPI transactions per day.
A UPI transaction may fail because of insufficient account balance.
Transactions may also fail because of incorrect UPI PIN or temporary service issues.
Customers should not repeatedly enter an incorrect UPI PIN.
After three incorrect UPI PIN attempts, UPI access is temporarily blocked.
Customers should wait 30 minutes before attempting UPI again after a PIN block.
Failed transactions should normally be checked in transaction history.
Customers can raise a complaint if a failed transaction is not resolved.
