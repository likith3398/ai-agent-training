NovaTrust Bank KYC Requirements
KYC verification is required before opening a NovaTrust customer account.
Customers must provide a valid government-issued identity document.
Customers must also provide a valid address document when required.
A recent customer photograph may be requested during verification.
The registered mobile number must be verified using an OTP.
KYC information must be kept accurate and updated.
Customers can update KYC information at a NovaTrust branch.
Additional documents may be requested based on the account type.
Incomplete KYC information may delay account activation.
Customers should never share OTPs or passwords with another person.
