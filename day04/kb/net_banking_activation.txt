NovaTrust Bank Net Banking Activation
Customers can activate net banking through the NovaTrust website.
The customer must have an active NovaTrust account.
The registered mobile number must be available for OTP verification.
The customer needs their account number and debit card details for registration.
A login password must be created during activation.
The login password must not be shared with anyone.
Customers can reset a forgotten password using the registered mobile number.
Three incorrect login attempts may temporarily lock online access.
Customers should contact NovaTrust support if access remains locked.
Net banking provides account viewing, transfers, statements, and service requests.
