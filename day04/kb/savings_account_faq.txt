NovaTrust Bank Savings Account FAQ
The NovaTrust Smart Savings Account requires a minimum opening deposit of ₹1,000.
The minimum monthly balance requirement is ₹1,000.
Customers receive a virtual debit card immediately after account activation.
The physical debit card is delivered within 7 working days.
Savings account interest is calculated daily and credited quarterly.
The standard interest rate for this synthetic account is 3.00% per year.
Customers can update their registered mobile number through a branch.
The account supports UPI, net banking, mobile banking, and ATM withdrawals.
For account-related complaints, customers should first contact NovaTrust support.
